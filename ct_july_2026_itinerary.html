Wadsworth Atheneum Museum of Art
Museum Kids Free U12
📍 600 Main St · Atheneum Square, Hartford, CT 06183

America's oldest continuously operating public art museum, founded in 1842 with nearly 50,000 works spanning 5,000 years. Five connected Gothic and Renaissance buildings form a castle-like complex in downtown Hartford. The collection ranges from Hudson River School masterpieces to world-renowned Surrealist paintings, plus a Cabinet of Art & Curiosity that kids love. Two massive, colorful Sol LeWitt wall murals on the grand staircases are reliably mesmerizing for young children.

🆓 Kids under 12 always free. On the second Saturday of each month, admission is free for everyone, with hands-on art activities and docent-led tours designed for families.
⏰ Open Wed–Fri 12–5pm · Sat–Sun 10am–5pm. Closed Mon & Tue — plan your arrival day around this.
🍽️ The BMUSE café on-site serves a full sit-down lunch with kid-friendly options (burgers, mac & cheese, chicken tenders). Great for a hot July afternoon.
🎠 Walk Atheneum Square and Bushnell Park right across the street — there's a restored 1914 Stein & Goldstein carousel kids can ride.

Jul 14–15
Castle Craig
Free Kids
📍 Hubbard Park, Peak Dr, Meriden, CT 06451

A stone lookout tower perched atop East Peak in Meriden — one of the best panoramic views in Connecticut. You can drive almost all the way to the top, making it very manageable with small kids. Climb the metal stairs inside the tower for a full 360° view across central CT on a clear day.

🆓 Completely free entry and parking. No tickets or reservations needed.
🚗 Drive up through Hubbard Park. Watch for speed bumps. Plenty of parking at the summit lot.
⏰ Open daily 10am–5:30pm. Pair with a picnic at Hubbard Park's lake below.

Jul 15–16
Yale Beinecke Rare Book Library
Free Library
📍 121 Wall St, New Haven, CT · Yale Campus

One of the world's great library buildings — its exterior panels of translucent Vermont marble filter natural light into a golden, glowing atmosphere inside. Even young kids are transfixed by the otherworldly architecture. The sunken sculpture garden outside is a great spot to let them run around after.

🆓 Free and open to the public. No reservation needed to visit the main gallery.
⏰ Mon–Tue & Thu 9am–7pm · Wed 10am–7pm · Fri 9am–5pm · Sat–Sun 12–5pm
🍕 New Haven pizza is legendary — Pepe's and Modern Apizza are 10 min away. Worth the wait in line.
🧙 Walk the Yale campus after — Gothic architecture great for Harry Potter fans of any age.

Jul 16–17
Hammonasset Beach State Park
Beach Kids
📍 1288 Boston Post Rd, Madison, CT · Long Island Sound

Connecticut's longest public beach — over two miles of wide, clean shoreline on the Long Island Sound. Gentle waves make it ideal for kids under 7. Beyond the beach there's a boardwalk nature trail through the marshes, a nature center, and a small snack stand on site.

🌅 Arrive by 8:30am in mid-July — parking fills completely by 10am on hot days.
🚿 Full changing rooms, rinse-off stations, and clean restrooms on site.
⏰ Open daily 8am–6:30pm. Small vehicle entry fee applies.
🦅 The lagoon trail and nature center on the far end are quiet and shaded — great bird watching break from the sun.

Jul 17–20
Saybrook Point Resort & Marina
Home Base Marina
📍 2 Bridge St, Old Saybrook, CT · CT River mouth

A beautiful resort right where the Connecticut River meets the Sound. Your shoreline home base for 3 nights — perfectly positioned for Gillette Castle, the Block Island ferry in New London, and day trips along Route 1. Indoor and outdoor pools, a full marina, and the acclaimed Fresh Salt restaurant on-site.

🏊 Indoor pool great for when kids need a break from the beach. Outdoor pool is heated.
🍽️ Fresh Salt restaurant has stunning marina views — reserve dinner in advance, it books up in summer.
📍 40 min to Gillette Castle · 30 min to New London ferry terminal for Block Island.

Jul 20–21
Gillette Castle State Park
State Park Kids
📍 67 River Rd, East Haddam, CT · Connecticut River

A genuinely eccentric stone castle perched above the Connecticut River, built by Victorian actor William Gillette (famous for playing Sherlock Holmes on stage). The interior is filled with bizarre wooden contraptions, hidden doors, and hand-carved details. Hiking trails with sweeping river views surround the grounds.

🎟️ Castle tours are seasonal — check the CT DEEP website before you go to confirm summer tour availability.
⛴️ A small ferry crosses the river right below the castle. Kids love watching it cross — you can ride it too.
🥾 Easy trails around the grounds are manageable for kids under 7. Great picnic area overlooking the river.

Jul 21–22
Block Island Day Trip
Ferry Island
📍 Ferry from New London, CT → Block Island, RI · ~70 min each way

A car-free island escape off the Rhode Island coast. The high-speed catamaran from New London takes just over an hour. Rent bikes at Old Harbor, swim at Crescent Beach, explore the lighthouse, and eat fried clams with a view. One of the best day trips in all of New England.

🎫 Book ferry tickets well in advance at goblockisland.com — July departures sell out weeks ahead. Passenger-only, no car needed.
🚲 Bike rentals right at the Old Harbor dock. Kids can ride in carrier bikes or tag-alongs.
🌊 The crossing can be choppy — pack motion sickness tablets for the little ones.
🏖️ Crescent Beach has calm, gentle waves — perfect for kids under 7.

Jul 23–26
Montréal, Québec
Canada 3 Nights
📍 Montréal, QC · ~5.5 hr drive from Old Saybrook

A world-class city that's incredibly welcoming to families with young kids. Mid-July brings warm weather, long evenings, and the city's famous festival season. The Biodôme, Old Port, and Mount Royal alone fill three full days — and the food scene (bagels, smoked meat, crêpes, poutine) is half the adventure.

Top Picks with Kids Under 7
🦎 Biodôme — Five ecosystems under one roof. Kids go wild. Allow 3–4 hours. Pair with the Botanical Garden or Insectarium next door at Olympic Park.
🎡 Old Port — La Grande Roue observation wheel, Clock Tower Beach, SOS Labyrinthe maze, cobblestone streets, crêpe stands.
🌿 Mount Royal Park — Kite flying, Beaver Lake picnics, Kondiaronk Belvedere views. Free, easy walking.
🎪 Complètement CiRQUE runs early–mid July with free outdoor circus shows in the Quartier des Spectacles — check if it overlaps.
🛂 Passports required for everyone including kids. Most places take card; keep some Canadian cash handy.

Planning Notes

Things to Book
in Advance

🎫 Book These ASAP

  • Block Island ferry tickets (goblockisland.com) — sell out weeks ahead in July
  • Saybrook Point Resort rooms — summer weekends fill fast
  • Montréal hotel — Old Port area most convenient
  • Fresh Salt restaurant at Saybrook for dinner

📋 Check Before You Go

  • Gillette Castle tour schedule (CT DEEP website) — seasonal hours
  • Wadsworth Atheneum closed Mon & Tue — schedule around this
  • Montréal CiRQUE festival overlap with your dates
  • Voiles en Voiles age minimums for kids under 7

🧳 Pack for This Trip

  • Passports for everyone (required for Montréal)
  • Reef shoes or water sandals for rocky shorelines
  • Motion sickness tablets for Block Island ferry
  • Sunscreen + reapplication sticks for beach days
  • Light layers for Montréal evenings

🚗 Driving from Middletown

  • → Wadsworth Atheneum (Hartford): ~20 min north
  • → Castle Craig (Meriden): ~20 min south
  • → New Haven (Beinecke + pizza): ~25 min south
  • → Hammonasset Beach: ~35 min south
  • → Old Saybrook / Saybrook Point: ~45 min south
  • → Montréal from Saybrook: ~5.5 hrs north

Have a wonderful trip.
