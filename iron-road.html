Useful rider features

Iron Road is designed to grow into a community route hub for motorcycle riders, from solo scouting to group rides.

★

Ratings and reviews

Let riders score curves, scenery, pavement quality, traffic, and beginner friendliness before committing to a route.

☁

Ride conditions

Add seasonal closures, weather warnings, road work, gravel alerts, and fuel-gap notes so nobody gets surprised.

☊

GPX import/export

Support GPS devices and trip-planning apps by importing GPX files and exporting completed routes for offline navigation.

⚑

Group ride planning

Publish meet-up times, pace, fuel stops, coffee stops, sweep riders, and emergency contacts for safer crew rides.
