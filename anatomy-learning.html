Core Body Systems

Memorize each system by function, key organs, and what happens when that system fails.

Skeletal

Support, structure, and protection.

  • Major organs: skull, vertebrae, ribs
  • Study focus: axial vs appendicular bones

Muscular

Movement and posture control.

  • Major organs: skeletal, smooth, cardiac muscle
  • Study focus: agonist-antagonist pairs

Nervous

Fast communication and control.

  • Major organs: brain, spinal cord, nerves
  • Study focus: CNS vs PNS pathways

Circulatory

Transports oxygen and nutrients.

  • Major organs: heart, arteries, veins
  • Study focus: pulmonary vs systemic flow

Respiratory

Gas exchange in the lungs.

  • Major organs: trachea, bronchi, alveoli
  • Study focus: ventilation and diffusion

Digestive

Breaks down and absorbs food.

  • Major organs: stomach, liver, intestines
  • Study focus: nutrient absorption sites

Urinary

Regulates fluid and electrolyte balance.

  • Major organs: kidneys, ureters, bladder
  • Study focus: nephron filtration steps

Endocrine

Hormonal signaling and metabolism.

  • Major organs: pituitary, thyroid, pancreas
  • Study focus: hormone target feedback loops
