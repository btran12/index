Core Body Systems

Memorize each system by function first, then connect each organ to that function.

Skeletal

Support, structure, and protection.

Muscular

Movement and posture control.

Nervous

Fast communication and control.

Circulatory

Transports oxygen and nutrients.

Respiratory

Gas exchange in the lungs.

Digestive

Breaks down and absorbs food.
