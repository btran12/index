Selected system

Skeletal system

Bones form the body's framework, protect delicate organs, and store minerals. Bone marrow also produces blood cells.

Key structures
Skull, spine, rib cage, pelvis, and limb bones

Follow the pathway
Axial bones protect the head and trunk; appendicular bones connect the limbs to the body's central axis.

Study cue
Trace from skull to spine and ribs, then out through the shoulder and hip girdles into the limbs.
